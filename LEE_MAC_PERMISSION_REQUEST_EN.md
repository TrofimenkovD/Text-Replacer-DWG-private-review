Subject: Permission to distribute a modified BFind adapter with Text-Replacer

Hello Lee,

I am the author/maintainer of Text-Replacer, a Windows desktop utility for
batch text replacement in Word, Excel, PDF and AutoCAD drawings.

A contributor created a non-interactive AutoLISP adapter derived from portions
of your Batch Find & Replace v2.0 program. The adapter:

- removes the DCL interface and directory-processing UI;
- receives one find/replace pair through environment variables;
- processes the drawing opened by AutoCAD or Core Console;
- searches model/layout space and block definitions;
- reports machine-readable replacement counts;
- adds support for literal AutoCAD Unicode sequences such as \U+0426;
- skips Excel-linked table cells;
- retains your copyright notice and clearly lists all modifications.

We would like to publish this modified adapter in the public MIT-licensed
Text-Replacer repository and distribute it with the free application. We will
keep your original copyright notice, link to your BFind page and terms, identify
the file as a modified derivative, and use any license wording you require.

Would you grant written permission for this public redistribution and
distribution with the application? If permission is conditional, please tell
us the exact attribution and license text you would like included.
